$20 Draft Battle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

$20 Draft Battle ("the app") is a two-player party game played on one device. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect it.

Information we collect

We don't collect anything ourselves. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers, and we never receive your name, email, contacts or photos. Ads are the one exception: see Advertising below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few things on your phone so it works the way you left it: player names you type, your settings (light or dark mode, holiday theme), your draft count, your daily streak and recently played categories. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Sharing

If you tap Share on the results screen, the app opens your phone's share menu with a text summary of the draft. You choose where it goes. The app doesn't send it anywhere on its own.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure them, Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location based on your IP address, ad and app interactions, and crash and performance data. On iPhone, you'll be asked for permission before any tracking, and you can change your choice at any time in your phone's Settings. Where privacy laws require it, you can also change your ad choices in the app under Settings. Learn more in Google's advertising policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
