Support

Found a bug or have a question? Email yp7buenapark@gmail.com and include your phone model if something isn't working.

How do I play?

Pick a category, set a budget and take turns raising or passing on each item. The How to Play tab has the full rules and lets you replay the tutorial.

Is the money real?

No. The $20 and $50 budgets are pretend. Nothing can be bought or won.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. There's no sign-up, and the game works offline. Both players share one phone.

How do I reset my stats and streak?

They're saved only on your phone, so deleting the app erases them.
